OpenServ · 2025 — 2026

The OpenServ launchpad

Taking AI-agent tokens on-chain on Base and Solana — with launches, liquidity and vesting designed for agents that have to last.

Role
Head of Blockchain — led development
When
Aug 2025 — Mar 2026
Chains
Base · Solana
Built with
Solidity · ERC-20 · Aerodrome Slipstream · Sablier · Meteora · TypeScript
(01)

Context

OpenServ is a full-stack platform for building and operating autonomous AI agents, powered by the SERV token. I joined as Head of Blockchain in August 2025, coming over from Eliza Labs, with a simple mandate: ship faster, harden the stack, and make OpenServ the easiest place for teams to launch AI-powered products.

The launchpad is where that mandate meets the chain: it lets projects built on OpenServ take their agent’s token on-chain.

(02)

The challenge

Agent tokens tend to fail in the same ways. Snipers take the first blocks, liquidity is thin or gets pulled, and team allocations unlock all at once. Teams building agents shouldn’t have to become DeFi engineers to avoid that — and they want to launch on more than one chain.

(03)

What I did

  • Led development of the OpenServ launchpad, taking AI-agent tokens on-chain for projects built on the platform.
  • Designed the launch mechanics, fee flows and tokenomics behind it, so every launch follows the same fair, liquid and long-term path.
  • Built it for more than one chain: Base first, with Solana launches alongside.
(04)

How a launch works

  1. 01BuildAn AI agent is built and run on OpenServ.
  2. 02LaunchA fixed-supply ERC-20 token goes live on Base — or on Solana.
  3. 03ProtectPhased anti-sniping keeps the opening fair.
  4. 04LiquidityAn Aerodrome Slipstream pool paired with WETH, with the LP locked long-term.
  5. 05AlignFees are shared with creators; team tokens vest on-chain through Sablier.

On Solana, launches run through Meteora’s Alpha Vault.

(05)

Outcome

The launchpad went live on Base first, with Solana launches following — giving every agent team on OpenServ a ready-made, repeatable path on-chain.
