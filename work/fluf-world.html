Non-Fungible Labs · 2021 — 2023

FLUF World & The Root Network

From sold-out Ethereum collections to a purpose-built layer 1 — the technical backbone of one of the early NFT ecosystems.

Role
Chief Technology Officer
When
Aug 2021 — May 2023
Chains
Ethereum · The Root Network
Built with
Solidity · ERC-721 · Merkle proofs · ECDSA · Substrate · Rust · Polkadot.js · XRPL
(01)

Context

FLUF World launched in August 2021 with 10,000 Flufs — ERC-721 collectibles on Ethereum that sold out in 40 minutes — and grew into an ecosystem: Party Bears, Burrows, Scenes & Sounds, Buzzies and more.

I joined Non-Fungible Labs as CTO that same month and led engineering through the company’s merger into Futureverse in December 2022.

(02)

The challenge

Every drop was a one-shot, high-stakes event: thousands of collectors hitting a contract in the same minutes, high-value airdrops to existing holders, and gas costs that made even small interactions — like renaming an NFT — painful. And Ethereum alone couldn’t carry the ecosystem’s ambitions.

(03)

What I did

  • Shipped the smart contracts behind dozens of ERC-721 mints: $50M+ in revenue, 30K+ international customers and 65K+ ETH of OpenSea volume.
  • Distributed high-value airdrops to thousands of holders with Merkle proofs, and used ECDSA signatures for gas-free interactions such as renaming.
  • Built custom Discord bots to take third-party bot risk out of the community.
  • Recruited, mentored and led a team of 20+ Web3 developers.
  • Led development on The Root Network, the Substrate-based layer 1 with native EVM that FLUF World architected in partnership with Ripple.
(04)

How it fits together

  1. 01MintERC-721 collections on Ethereum.
  2. 02AirdropHolders claim with Merkle proofs.
  3. 03SignECDSA signatures make interactions like renaming gas-free.
  4. 04ScaleThe Root Network: a Substrate L1 with native EVM and XRP as gas.
  5. 05BridgeLinks to XRPL and Ethereum, and a JS/TS API on Polkadot.js for developers.
(05)

Outcome

By Ripple’s announcement in June 2022, the ecosystem counted 14 collections, 195,000+ NFTs minted and 340,000+ transactions. In December 2022, Non-Fungible Labs joined the eight-company merger that formed Futureverse.
